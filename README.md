# GroundTruth
Design-stage project: a proposed motion-compensated smart cane attachment for ground-level hazard sensing and haptic warnings. AI-assisted concept; prototype and testing not yet started.

A Motion-Compensated Smart Cane Attachment for Ground-Level Hazard Sensing and Haptic Warnings

1. Project Overview

GroundTruth is a proposed retrofit attachment for a conventional white cane. It aims to provide additional information about selected ground-level hazards, such as drops, steps, and potholes, through sensor-based sensing and haptic warnings.

The goal is to explore whether motion compensation can help interpret ground measurements while the cane is being swept.

2. Proposed Approach

The design concept includes:

- A multi-zone time-of-flight (ToF) sensor for ground-depth measurements.
- An inertial measurement unit (IMU) to estimate cane motion.
- An ESP32-S3 microcontroller for processing.
- Haptic feedback through a vibration actuator.
- Proposed contact-based surface sensing as an experimental extension.

These are proposed design elements, not verified hardware implementations.

7. Safety and Limitations

GroundTruth is intended to supplement, not replace, a conventional white cane. A missing warning must not be interpreted as proof that the path is safe. Reliability, environmental limitations, battery safety, and usability require evaluation before any real-world use.
